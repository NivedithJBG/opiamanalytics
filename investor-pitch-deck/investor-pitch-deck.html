Cloud Computing and API’s

On the one hand, cloud computing has changed the entire business model of software delivery, leading to global delivery of software at lower cost while reducing the cost of obtaining a software by customers. This has made the market huge and wide. Software such as data analytics, once only accessible to Fortune 500 companies, are today available to small and medium scale companies at an affordable subscription price making these companies perform like any of the leading companies in the world.

Challenges facing the industry

90% of the project managers do not have formal knowledge on what drives performance and therefore they do no not know where action is required to deliver projects on time and at targeted cost.
70% of the management have no visibility to KPI’s of projects as there is limited access to data. Decisions are still based on intuitions and thumb rules.
Delayed deliveries, cost overruns, unhappy customers, loss of markets and litigations.
When time and cost are all the more critical, traditional tools and methods of project management have not proven effective for project managers and management.
Most companies are currently using multiple systems, complex processes, and outdated technology with no visible improvement in efficiency.
There is repository of data, but there are no data analysts or BI tools to turn that data into actionable information and insights.
