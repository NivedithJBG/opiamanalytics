Managed Services

Infrastructure as a service

Cloud computing was a game changer. It enables organisations to get rid of all hardware that stored and processed data. It enabled us to move anywhere and still could perform any work using any connected device at any time. We need not worry anymore about down time, power cuts, breakdown or even security. The availability is 24x7.

That was a quantum leap in computing. No more investments in servers or data centres, no more ongoing cost of maintenance and people and what’s more, you can scale your requirement of computing power and storage as and when your demand goes up. It’s time organisations run on cloud. We enable organisations to move into cloud and we even maintain them 24x7 without you ever worrying about it.

Software as a service

Cloud computing enabled a plethora of applications to be built on cloud. Today you have access to world class technologies available to a Fortune 500 company at a small subscription cost.

Opiam is deployed in cloud, you can access it at the cost of the salary of a normal employee. The value is huge. In addition to reduction in the size of the head count, there is huge value in terms operational efficiency resulting in breakthrough strategic and financial results. There is hardly any initial investment. There is no more legacy systems. Your software is updated to the current technology as it evolves at a minimal cost so that your organisation is always up-to-date on technology. All the more you can terminate it anytime you feel like.

Opiam is designed simple, easy to learn and quick to deploy.
